Why hasn't it gone away?

Pain is almost never where the problem starts.

Past a certain point, though, these adaptations lead to changes in the body. Pain is the signal.

It shouldn't be suppressed.

The system should be restored to working the way it's meant to.

Frequently asked questions

What many people ask.

When will I feel results?

Most people feel the first changes — easier breathing, better posture awareness, less tension — within 2–3 weeks. A more significant, lasting reduction in pain usually comes within 4–6 weeks. It all depends on how consistently you do the exercises at home.

How is it different from conventional physiotherapy?

Conventional physiotherapy is mostly protocol-based: a given diagnosis gets a given set of exercises. The TestKözéppont Method starts from your individual biomechanics — we examine how your pelvis is shifted and your asymmetries, and build a program around them. The goal is for you to understand how your own body works, not just do what you're told.

Can I come with a herniated disc or other disc problems?

I've worked successfully with herniated discs and other disc problems. During an active, acute inflammatory phase or right after surgery I can't help — that calls for medical care. If you're unsure, we'll find out in the consultation whether the program is right for you.

What happens in the first session?

About 90–120 minutes. First we go through your complaints and history in detail. Then comes the biomechanical assessment — we look at how your pelvis is shifted, your asymmetries and your movement patterns. You'll understand what the problem is and why. Finally, I teach you the exercises for the first 4 weeks and we record the videos.

Why trust me?

For eight years I struggled with chronic lower back pain and a neck that kept locking up. The solution didn't come from yet another treatment, but from understanding how the body works as a whole. As an architect, I learned that a building's stability is determined by its foundations — the body works the same way: when its centre, the pelvis, tips out of balance, the whole system suffers. With the mentoring of a physiotherapist, this became the TestKözéppont Method — built on anatomical and biomechanical foundations, restoring function instead of masking symptoms.
